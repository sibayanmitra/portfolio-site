LLM interpretability · evaluation · safety

I study what language models represent, and when that changes what they do.

Most of my work sits between mechanistic interpretability and behavioural evaluation: finding directions and circuits inside a model, then testing whether they actually drive behaviour in agentic and multi-turn settings.
